## Core Research Reports

- Bybit Alpha: Bybit’s Web3 evolution enabling walletless, gasless on-chain trading funded by UTA balance. Features liquidity farming (DeFi yields), Alpha Radar for new tokens, and points tasks. Opportunity: frictionless DeFi onboarding within a CEX-secured stack. Risks: Bybit ecosystem dependency and market-driven yield variability.
- Altura (ALU): Web3 gaming platform supporting AI-infused games and NFT-based in-game items with APIs/SDKs across chains. Token utility in governance/staking. Opportunity: expanding game-NFT market. Risks: intense competition (e.g., Enjin) and uncertain game adoption.
- Rails: Hybrid perps platform combining CEX-level speed (sub-ms execution) with DeFi self-custody on a privacy-preserving ZK L2 and on-chain custody. Opportunity: strong perps demand and points-to-TGE funnel (TGE planned 2026-02). Risks: regulatory headwinds and liquidity bootstrapping.
- Brevis: ZK coprocessor and cross-chain data proving platform enabling verifiable historical data computation and interoperability. ProverNet decentralizes proof markets. Opportunity: rollup interoperability and on-chain data composability; $BREV staking. Risks: ZK complexity and competition (e.g., Succinct).
- Kindred AI: Agentic AI protocol using licensed IP to create emotionally aware companions with multimodal interfaces. Opportunity: non–zero-sum “time economy” for companionship. Risks: privacy controls and IP licensing dependencies.
- Polymarket: Leading decentralized prediction market (Polygon stack) with non-custodial USDC settlement and deep event liquidity (elections, macro). Opportunity: real-world event coverage and liquidity network effects. Risks: regulatory constraints and market manipulation vectors.
- Kalshi: US CFTC-regulated event market with legal access for mainstream users; focuses on event contracts including economics and sports. Opportunity: regulatory moat. Risks: rule changes and competition from decentralized venues.
- Opinion Labs: Decentralized prediction market and oracle system with ZK-based settlement and consensus-driven oracles. Opportunity: transparent event trading integrated with AI agents. Risks: oracle accuracy and liquidity fragmentation.
- pDAI (PulseChain): Community-driven stablecoin fork on PulseChain targeting a $1 peg; historically volatile and subject to depegs. Narrative includes potential Liberty Swap efforts to re-peg and tie-ins with UNI/Aave governance. Risks: peg sustainability and governance centralization concerns.
- SANTA Browser: Web3 browser with privacy, built-in wallets, and dApp integrations; rewards frameworks (cashback, points, airdrops) to drive engagement. Opportunity: onboarding mainstream users with incentive loops. Risks: user retention and security surface of bundled features.
- spaace_io: Gamified, multi-chain NFT marketplace with revenue-sharing and community incentives; fair-launch mechanics and leaderboard-based rewards. Opportunity: creator-aligned incentives and multi-chain reach. Risks: NFT market cyclicality.
- Billions Network: Privacy and age-assurance–focused network with a planned TGE (targeting Q1 2026 window) and a large user base. Opportunity: compliance-native privacy rails. Risks: regulatory shifts and adoption beyond initial niches.
- idOS Network: Decentralized, self-sovereign identity layer for reusable KYC/credentials across chains. Opportunity: composable identity for stablecoin/DeFi integrations. Risks: privacy/UX trade-offs and adoption inertia.
- SoSoValueCrypto: AI-driven research platform with EXP-point seasons and tokenized rewards (30M $SOSO airdrop Q1 2026). Opportunity: incentivized learning and research distribution. Risks: token economics and engagement sustainability.
- robox.to: Robotics and intelligent automation platform on BNB Chain; early-user airdrops and agent-focused ecosystem. Opportunity: Web3-native automation primitives. Risks: real utility vs speculation and technical maturity.
- dropee_app: Telegram-native tap-to-earn with points-to-TGE funnel; community allocation 10–15%. Opportunity: low-friction user acquisition. Risks: retention post-TGE and token sink design.
- IOPn: L1 focused on sovereign AI, identity, and RWA tokenization; compliance-friendly EVM chain with scaled throughput. Opportunity: regulated market integrations. Risks: L1 competition and developer ecosystem growth.
