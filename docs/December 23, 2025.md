## **Core Research Reports**

- **AlignerZ Labs**: Base-native launchpad introducing IWO (commitment-weighted allocations) and TVS (tradable vesting NFTs). Pros: mitigates post-TGE dumps, stabilizes liquidity, rewards long-term users. Cons: vesting may deter short-term farmers; early adoption risk. Potential: High for fairer, aligned launches.
- **HeyElsaAI (Elsa)**: AI crypto copilot with native-language support; rewards real users via airdrops/points to test belief and decentralize ownership. Pros: clear alignment design and UX focus. Cons: needs scalable traction beyond hype. Potential: Medium–High with tangible product updates and tasks.
- **DegenAI**: Text-to-script AI trading for HIP-3 market-making. Pros: accessible no-code automation, activity can be both profitable and airdrop-eligible. Cons: algo risk in volatile regimes. Potential: High for systematic traders.
- **OP NET**: Bitcoin-centric L2 with early testing paths that may map to future distributions. Pros: leverages BTC brand and user base. Cons: fierce L2 competition and BTC-led volatility. Potential: Medium–High for BTC ecosystem farmers.
- **INFO Web3 Media**: Decentralized media stack prioritizing privacy, content ownership, and incentives; Chinese-language expansion via ATV. Pros: fills a niche in decentralized media. Cons: adoption hurdles and network effects. Potential: Medium.
- **FractionAI**: Community-driven agent evolution through gamified sessions. Pros: rapid iteration from feedback loops. Cons: tempo may rely on events/sentiment. Potential: Medium in AI–Web3 crossover.
- **GiftHorse (TON)**: Social wishlist and contribution platform; missions, referrals, and AP aimed at future $GHT distribution. Pros: low barrier, social loops. Cons: adoption and airdrop dilution risks. Potential: Medium.
- **Brevis_zk**: ZK co-processor enabling smart contracts to access and verify arbitrary historical on-chain data with programmable computations. Pros: “unlimited memory” + verifiable compute; speed emphasis. Cons: infra complexity and integrator learning curves. Potential: High for data-rich dApps.
- **Bybit Alpha Farm**: CEX-integrated CLMM LP product with stables and volatile pools (77%–2060% APR). Pros: unified trading account, fees + incentives, higher capital efficiency. Cons: IL and range management complexity. Potential: Medium–High for advanced LPs.
- **Opinion Labs**: Decentralized prediction/opinion markets with a consensus oracle; multi-billion cumulative volume since 2024. Pros: transparent, objective market resolution. Cons: regulatory and information asymmetry challenges. Potential: Medium–High.
- **palu (Meme)**: BSC meme coin tied to Binance branding; high volatility and community-driven narrative with recent underperformance. Pros: brand adjacency. Cons: speculative, unstable price action. Potential: Low–Medium.
- **YO MAMA ($YMA)**: Base-chain meme focused on humor/roast culture. Pros: entertainment-driven virality. Cons: micro-cap risk and limited fundamentals. Potential: Low.
- **$NIGHT (Midnight Network)**: Governance token supporting DUST generation; active Launchpool demand despite emissions. Pros: strong exchange backing, privacy and perps angle. Cons: tokenomics/airdrops may pressure price. Potential: Medium.
- **$NOTIFAI**: Utility token powering notification/alert tooling (e.g., market cap alerts) with growing holder base. Pros: practical use-case. Cons: crowded tooling market. Potential: Medium.
- **PropTory (RWA Real Estate)**: Tokenizes property into compliant digital rights with transparent yield flows. Pros: aligns with on-chain RWA adoption. Cons: legal/regulatory fragmentation. Potential: Medium–High.
- **ApeXdex**: High-performance DEX for perps/spot with low fees and self-custody; modular, intent-centric, chain-agnostic design. Pros: latency and liquidity focus. Cons: DEX competition and CEX UX expectations. Potential: Medium–High.
- **Beldex**: Privacy-centric L1 + app suite with $BDX. Pros: tailwind from privacy narrative. Cons: regulatory headwinds. Potential: Medium.
- **DropHUB Airdrop**: Zero-fee prediction markets rewarding creators and high-volume traders. Pros: low barrier and clear activity incentives. Cons: prediction market volatility and outcome risk. Potential: Low–Medium for opportunistic farmers.
- **General Farming Airdrops**: Token locks, compounding strategies, and diversified positioning can lead to outsized distributions. Pros: cumulative reward potential. Cons: contract risk and long lockups. Potential: High when diversified and security-aware.
